**Closed at [M5-08](../tasks/M5-08-acceptance-and-tag.md), archived on time — the fourth milestone
running.** M0's and M1's logs were archived a milestone late and at 381 KB between them; M2's was the
first done at its own acceptance, M3's the second, M4's the third, and this is the fourth.

Durable lessons were promoted **before** these entries moved, which is rule 5's ordering: toolchain traps
to [Traps.md](../../Traps.md), rules the code depends on to [Architecture.md §18](../../Architecture.md#18-invariants),
and obligations with an owning task to the [M6 carry-forward ledger](../ROADMAP.md#carry-forward-into-m6).
What is below is the index of what happened, not the rules that came out of it.

**Every *Verified* row is inside its own entry**, one line each, and the chain of counts runs
**2 197 → 2 521 EditMode** and **16 → 21 PlayMode** across the milestone.

**The milestone in one line:** the game stopped being one character — a card per class at the door, a
Gravecaller behind it with a led projectile, a blink that leaves a corpse the arena walks at, and an army
that rises from a quarter of the dead; twelve nodes of its own, and at half its tree a run stops and
borrows one branch of the other class for good (CH §5.4).

**And M5 is the first milestone whose acceptance was measured rather than argued.** M5-08 instrumented a
build, the owner played two runs to stages 16 and 19 killing three bosses each, and the log answered three
ledger rows that had been carried since M3 — including one, [row 8](../ROADMAP.md#carry-forward-into-m6),
that three acceptances in a row had named as their own. It also found two defects that no amount of
reading had: a boss tuned outside its own design band, and a splash screen offering branches the model
refuses. Both are recorded below and in [M5-08a](../tasks/M5-08a-splash-offers-what-install-refuses.md).

### Carry-forward into M3

**What M3's specs must absorb.** Opened at M2-15 with eight rows, on [M2's terms](ROADMAP-M2.md#carry-forward-into-m2): every row names the task that must deal with it, and a row leaves only when its owner's *As built* says it is answered. **No row was carried from M2 — all fourteen closed** — so these are new: five are misses or measurements from M2-15's own checklist, two are parking-lot entries that have acquired an owner, and one is the open decision M2 could not make for itself. **Row 9 was opened by M3-00b**, which is what a spec group is for: writing a screen found a thing the milestone cannot judge itself on. Ranked by what it costs to fix later rather than now. **Two are already closed:** row 3 by M2-15a before any spec was written, row 7 by M3-00a with a hook; rows 1 and 2 have their arithmetic and their fields from M3-00a and wait on the tasks that build them. **M3-00c moved four rows and closed none**, which is what a screen group does to a ledger: **row 1 now has a table** and closes at 3 / 4 / 5 hits on paper (M3-12c rule 8), **row 2 gained two more bumps** including the first ever profile migration, **row 5 has an owner and a rule** about not blessing a regression, and **rows 6 and 9 both roughly doubled** — eight colour readers across five files, and five text readers of which one cannot be fixed by localisation at all.

**M3-00d moved five rows, closed none on paper, and gave two of them a task that did not exist.** Rows **6** and **9** were both recounted against the code and both were wrong in the same direction — understated: row 6 is **nine** readers across nine files, not eight across five, because the count had been tracking M3's own screens on top of three pre-existing files and had never named `EnemyHitFeedback`'s hit flash; row 9 is **seven** readers, not five, because M3-10a's slot buttons were listed as an art problem when a word fixes them. **M3-15 closed it: seven of nine struck, three carried, none dropped.** Struck, each by the *As built* the mechanism demands: **2** (M3-09c — `PlayerProfile` v2, the first profile step `MigrateProfile` has ever run), **3** (M2-15a, before any M3 spec existed), **5** (M3-11c — fourteen rows, behaviour unchanged), **6** (M3-13a — nine readers, one file, and `Danger_IsUsedByNothingElse` as the rule that keeps it shut), **7** (M3-00a's hook, **re-probed at M3-15 rather than assumed**: a throwaway staged `ProjectSettings/` file was refused with the Traps §5 pointer, and accepted under `ALLOW_PROJECT_SETTINGS=1`), **9**'s structural half (M3-14b — every key in every asset sweeps green), and **1**'s arithmetic half (M3-12c's 3 / 4 / 5, **re-measured at M3-15 off the shipped assets through the real curve rather than copied**, reproducing `TimeToKillTests` exactly).

**The three that carry, carry for one shared reason rather than three, and it is worth naming because it is new: they all wanted a number a playtest produces, and the acceptance playtest could not produce one.** The owner's verdict was that the UI is **too cramped to read and too unfinished to feel** — so row **1**'s played hit counts, row **8**'s two labelled timings and row **9**'s two-second read were not ticked on a shrug, they were carried. Judging twelve descriptions on a 44 dp cell is judging the cell. **This is the first time in this project's life a ledger row has been carried because of a *readout* rather than an unbuilt mechanism**, and it is why [M4's table](../ROADMAP.md#carry-forward-into-m4) opens with the UI instead of the boss. Row **4** carries whole, as it always was going to.

**One thing this table predicted wrongly, recorded because the prediction is the useful part.** Row 8 and the parking lot both named CH §5.2's exponent as *the* pre-identified tuning change and said only a stopwatch could settle it. M3-15 measured it through the shipped `XpCurve` at its real `ToReach(Level + 1)` threshold and **did not change it**: 1.4 gives **L8 at stage 5 and L14 at stage 10** against the table's *1–8* and *~13*, so the curve already agrees where the checklist looks, and it is **stage 20** where they part (L27 against ~22). **The doc was corrected and the asset was not.** And the cost the row never considered: both candidate exponents (1.5 and 1.6) push **stage-15 TTK from 4 hits to 5** — inside GD §12.4's band, but on its ceiling with no headroom until M7-04's twenty-seven nodes.

| **1** *(arithmetic closed, played half carried to [M4 row 2](../ROADMAP.md#carry-forward-into-m4))* | **The TTK invariant breaks at stage 15, not at stage 30 where M2-15 looked.** GD §12.4 wants a basic enemy dead in 3–5 hits *at every depth*. GD §12.5 wants +8–12 % effective DPS per stage from the tree, and **that target is now a number this row can be checked against** rather than a sentence. The danger is shipping a tree tuned by feel that happens not to close it, and discovering at M4 that stage 20 is unkillable for reasons nobody connects to the tree. **M3-00a did the arithmetic** ([M3-01a](../tasks/M3-01a-xp-and-levels.md) rule 9): under the shipped curve the player gets **≈ 1.3 picks a stage from 1 to 30**, so GD §12.5's +8–12 % a stage means **the average pick is worth ≈ +6–9 % effective DPS**, or the damage-touching half of the tree ≈ +12–18 % each; a Husk needs +2 % / +70 % at stage 15 and +52 % / +153 % at stage 30 to die in five / three hits. M3-12 authors against those numbers and owes a TTK test at 1 / 15 / 30. **M3-00c did that arithmetic against a concrete twelve-node tree and it closes** ([M3-12c](../tasks/M3-12c-oathbound-tree-v1.md) rule 8): **3 hits at stage 1, 4 at stage 15, 5 at stage 30**, inside GD §12.4's band at all three. **The reason it closes is Overflow, not the tree** — a twelve-node tree fills at level 13 (≈ stage 9), so stage 30's level 42 leaves **29 Overflow levels** rather than M3-08a rule 8's fourteen, and the damage multiplier is ×1.73 against the +52 % row 1 asks for. That is CH §5.2 working, and it is also the warning: a small tree makes Overflow do most of the work, and M7-04's full twenty-seven shifts it back. **M3-12c rule 9 states what the test excludes and why** — reach, cone width, knockback, both Actives and fire rate all raise effective DPS without raising damage per hit, and a test that counted them would pass while the tree did nothing. **M3-00d gave the row its checklist and added the structural half nobody had claimed** ([M3-14b](../tasks/M3-14b-content-validation.md) rule 9): M3-08a rule 2 named M3-14 as the task that must refuse a tree which can *starve* — blocked but not full, so a pick becomes Overflow through an authoring mistake rather than through CH §5.2 — and that check is now specced (exhaustive at twelve nodes, sampled and stated as such above twelve). [M3-15](../tasks/M3-15-acceptance-and-tag.md) carries three rows for this one: the played hit counts at 1 / 15 / 30, the `Ttk_UnlevelledStageThirtyStillFails` control, and **the warning recorded whatever the verdict** — the band holds at stage 30 on 29 Overflow levels against twelve nodes, so a *"closed"* that does not say so hands M7-04 a surprise. It also states the inverse failure: **if a twelve-node build is unkillable at stage 30 the row is not closed, it is inverted**, and GD §12.5's *"everything in §12 is decorative"* is the cost. **M3-01a built the curve half and pinned both ends of it in tests rather than in prose:** `Pacing_LevelEightByStageFive` asserts the level CH §5.2's table and its own formula agree on, and `Pacing_TreeFullByStageTwenty` asserts the level at which they stop agreeing — 27 against the table's 22 — so the divergence this row's arithmetic rests on is now a red test if anyone retunes the exponent rather than a paragraph anyone can miss. The rest of the row is unchanged and still open: the numbers M3-12 authors against were computed from these two, and nothing yet raises a player's damage. **M3-10b built the only thing that ever tells the player half of that answer happened, and the row still does not close.** This row's own arithmetic puts Overflow at ≈ +28 % damage and +28 % max HP by stage 30 against the +52 % a Husk needs — M3-00c's recount takes it to **29** Overflow levels and ×1.73 on a twelve-node tree, which is *more than half* of the fix — and until this task nothing in the game said a word about any of it. A toast reading *"Overflow ×N"* is now the whole of the announcement; it carries the **running total** rather than the increment because the total is the part that means something (M3-10b rule 10), and it appears over a running fight with no pause, which is M3-08a rule 7 honoured rather than reopened. **What it does not do is raise a number.** The row closes when M3-12a's stats and M3-12c's nodes exist and its TTK test runs; what this task changes is that the Overflow half will be *visible* when they do rather than silently correct, which is the difference between CH §5.2's *"levelling never stops meaning something"* being true and being true in secret. **M3-11a-ii moves this row by exactly nothing, and says so on purpose.** Bulwark is the first active in the game and it deals **no damage**: it is survivability, so it moves the *other* side of GD §12.4 — the death horizon and the guardrails — and **M3-12's TTK test must not count it**. The row is a time-to-kill question with a formula on both sides, and a 35-point shield changes neither side of it.

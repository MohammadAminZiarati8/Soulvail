# FX-04 — Ground warnings stay above the floor

**Size:** S · **Depends on:** — · **Branch:** `dev`
**Design refs:** owner rule "readable at first look" (2026-11-03)

## Goal

Every flat boss warning is drawn at one shared height that clears the Jungle floor's bumps, so none sinks into the ground.

Owner playtest, 2026-11-05: Thornheart's red danger band went under the rocky ground. The flat warnings sit 3–6 cm above the boss's feet (`ThornheartView` band 0.04, pods 0.06; `ThornweaverView` 0.03; `GravemawView` ring 0.05, crack 0.06; `WarlordView` wedge 0.03), and floor rocks rise above that.

## Behaviour

1. **First, measure:** in the Editor, take the Jungle arena floor meshes used by stages 20 and 25. Measure the highest walkable-floor vertex above the arena's base height, inside the NavMesh bounds. Report it in *As built*.
2. `GroundLift.Height` = that bump + 0.02 m, rounded up to 0.01, and no lower than 0.06. A test asserts it clears every Jungle floor mesh.
3. Where two warnings share a spot (Thornheart band and pod ring), the later-drawn one keeps its existing ordering. Use render queue or sort order, not a second height.
4. No view keeps its own ground-warning height constant. A warning that sits **above** `Height` on purpose (`SprayConeView`, `ShockwaveView`, `ChainLinkViews`, traps) is untouched.

## Manual verification (Editor / device)

1. **[Editor]** Stage 20 Thornheart: the strangle band, pod rings and spokes stay visible across the whole arena, rocks included.
2. **[Editor]** Stages 15, 24, 25: the Warlord wedge, Thornweaver walls and Gravemaw rings/cracks are visible over the rocks.
3. **[device]** The same three bosses read at a glance on a phone.

## Out of scope

- ZTest-Always or stencil materials; if the measured bump exceeds 0.2 m, stop and report instead of lifting.
- Non-Jungle arenas; warning colours or shapes.

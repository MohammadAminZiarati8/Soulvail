# JG-13b — Buds guard their mother

**Size:** M · **Depends on:** JG-13a (both edit `MotherPitcherBehaviour.cs`) · **Branch:** `dev` (direct)
**Design refs:** GD §9.1 rule 4, GD §16.4; JG-12b rule 4, JG-12h rule 3

## Goal

A bud stays a passive shield but turns to face Mother Pitcher and idles in a clip of its own, a lean
toward her with leaves raised and a slow pulse, so it reads as guarding her.

## Behaviour

1. **`Guard`, authored in `pitcher.py`.** 60 frames at 30 fps, looping, first and last pose equal, the
   root still. The jug leans toward its front (the eyes' side) 12–18° at frames 15 and 45 and stands
   at 0 and 30; the leaf arms rise and reach forward on each lean; the `jug` bone scales 1.0 → 1.08 →
   1.0 with each lean (a pulse). `Idle`, `Shuffle`, `Attack`, `Hit`, `Death` keep their names and lengths.
2. **A bud idles in `Guard`; a Pitcher does not.** `AC_PitcherBud` overrides only `Idle` with
   `Guard`. `Pitcher_Bud.prefab` is a `Pitcher.prefab` variant whose Animator uses it;
   `PitcherBud.asset`'s body is that prefab, still Static, scale 0.6, no contact damage.
   `Pitcher.prefab` keeps `AC_Pitcher`.
3. **Buds turn to her.** Each Mother tick, every live bud gets one `EnemyMoveIntent`: zero velocity,
   facing the unit XZ direction from the bud to her. Her own intent is unchanged.
   `Mother_Tick_AllocatesNothing` still passes with three buds.
